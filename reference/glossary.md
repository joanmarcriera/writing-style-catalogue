# Glossary

## Writing & Communication Terms

**BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)**
Answer or recommendation in the first sentence. Supporting details follow.

**Smart Brevity**
Scannable format with headline, 3–5 key points, and supporting context. Readable in 30 seconds.

**Minto Pyramid Principle**
Conclusion first, then 3 supporting arguments, then evidence. Used for logical recommendations.

**ADR (Architecture Decision Record)**
Document recording a significant technical decision, context, and rationale.

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)**
Step-by-step document ensuring consistent process execution.

**Runbook**
Operational procedure for responding to specific situations (incidents, troubleshooting).

**Business Case**
Financial and strategic justification for investment or change.

**Gross Margin**
Revenue minus cost of goods sold, expressed as percentage of revenue.

**NRR (Net Revenue Retention)**
Revenue retained from existing customers plus expansion revenue, minus churn.

**ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**
Predictable annual revenue from subscriptions or recurring contracts.

**Payback Period**
Time required for investment returns to equal initial investment.

## Document Types

**PRD (Product Requirement Document)**
Specification of product features, requirements, and acceptance criteria.

**Board Paper**
Comprehensive governance document for board decision-making.

**Executive Briefing**
Formal summary for C-level executives with context, analysis, and recommendation.

**Memo**
Internal document communicating information or requesting decision/action.

**FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)**
Compiled questions and answers addressing common topics.

## Audience & Context Terms

**C-Suite**
Executive leadership (CEO, CFO, CTO, etc.)

**Stakeholder**
Anyone affected by or interested in a decision or outcome.

**Subject Matter Expert**
Person with deep knowledge in a specific domain.
