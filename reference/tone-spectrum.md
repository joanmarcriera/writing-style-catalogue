# Tone Spectrum

Different writing contexts require different tones. Choose your tone deliberately.

## Tone Spectrum (Formal ↔ Casual)

**Very Formal**
Legal documents, formal policy, board papers
- Language: "Shall," "herein," "aforementioned"
- Structure: Numbered sections, defined terms
- Example: "The Party of the First Part shall indemnify..."

**Formal**
Executive briefings, tax notes, professional communications
- Language: Clear but measured, no contractions
- Structure: Organised with headers and sections
- Example: "We recommend approval of the capital investment."

**Professional**
Business emails, memos, consulting reports
- Language: Clear, direct, professional but accessible
- Structure: Logical but not overly rigid
- Example: "We should migrate to the cloud. Here's why..."

**Conversational**
Internal updates, team communications, some blog posts
- Language: Natural, can use contractions, friendly
- Structure: Relaxed but still organised
- Example: "Here's what happened last week..."

**Casual**
Social media, internal Slack, very informal contexts
- Language: Natural speech patterns, emoji acceptable
- Structure: Minimal formalisation
- Example: "Just shipped the new dashboard! 🎉"

## Tone Qualities

**Confident vs. Cautious**
- Confident: "This will save £1M annually"
- Cautious: "This could potentially save around £1M annually"
- Choose confident for decisions; cautious when uncertain

**Direct vs. Diplomatic**
- Direct: "This approach won't work"
- Diplomatic: "We might explore alternative approaches"
- Choose direct for close teams; diplomatic for sensitive contexts

**Formal vs. Personal**
- Formal: "The team has completed the project"
- Personal: "We've completed the project"
- Choose "we" for internal; formal for external

## Tone & Audience

| Audience | Appropriate Tone |
|----------|-----------------|
| C-Suite | Formal, confident, concise |
| Board | Formal, measured, comprehensive |
| Engineering Team | Professional, technical, direct |
| Sales Team | Conversational, positive, motivational |
| Customers | Professional, helpful, clear |
| Public | Accessible, clear, professional |

---

Tone affects credibility and impact. Choose deliberately, not by accident.
