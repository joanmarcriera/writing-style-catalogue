# Recommended Resources for Further Learning

External resources that complement this catalogue.

## Writing Style & Communication

**Books:**
- "Made to Stick" – Chip & Dan Heath (clarity, memorability)
- "HBR Guide to Better Business Writing" (executive writing)
- "Resonate" – Nancy Duarte (storytelling, presentations)
- "The Sense of Style" – Steven Pinker (clear writing)

**Online Resources:**
- Write the Docs community (technical writing)
- Gov.uk content guide (plain English principles) – verify online before relying on availability
- Medium's Better Marketing publication (writing for audiences)

## Presentation Skills

**Resources:**
- "Steal the Show" – Michael Port (public speaking)
- "Presentation Zen" – Garr Reynolds (visual design)
- TED Talks (study effective presentations)

## Strategic Communication

**Resources:**
- "Thinking, Fast and Slow" – Daniel Kahneman (decision-making, persuasion)
- "Influence" – Robert Cialdini (persuasion principles)
- "The Goal" – Eliyahu Goldratt (problem-solving storytelling)

## Technical Writing

**Resources:**
- "Technical Writing for Software" by Kieran Morgan – verify online before relying on availability
- Write the Docs community
- API documentation examples (analyse well-designed API docs)

## Professional Development

**Resources:**
- Coursera or edX courses in technical writing
- Toastmasters for public speaking
- Writing workshops and boot camps

---

**Note**: Verify online resources before relying on availability. Recommendations are current as of publication date.
