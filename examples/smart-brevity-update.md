# Smart Brevity Update Example

**Q2 Performance Summary – Revenue 23% Above Target**

• Q2 revenue: £3.2M (23% above forecast)
• Gross margin: 70% (up 2 points YoY)
• New customers: 18 (net addition: 15)
• Churn: 1.2% (down from 2.1% last quarter)

**What Drove It**
Enterprise segment exceeded expectations. Three new enterprise customers signed totalling £800k ARR. Mid-market segment grew steadily at 15% with strong retention.

**Challenges Ahead**
One major customer delayed renewal decision (£200k ARR). Sales cycle lengthening in new markets (4 months vs. 3 months expected). Support costs tracking higher than budget.

**Next Quarter Priorities**
Resolve delayed customer renewal by end July. Launch new product feature in August. Expand into EMEA market (exploratory meetings starting).

---

**Key principles shown:**
- Headline captures the story
- 3 key facts (not 5+)
- Supporting context in one paragraph
- Clear challenges identified
- Next steps are specific

**Word count:** ~130 words (scannable in 30 seconds)
