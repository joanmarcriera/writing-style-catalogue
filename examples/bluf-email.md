# BLUF Email Example

**Subject: Approval Needed – Cloud Migration Investment (£600k)**

We should approve a £600k investment in cloud infrastructure migration. This delivers £1.5M in annual savings starting Year 2 and reduces our dependency on ageing on-premises systems.

**Business case:**
• Annual operating savings: £1.5M (vs. renewal cost of £3.6M over 5 years)
• Payback period: 18 months
• Risk: Low (phased approach; external partner support)
• Timeline: 18-month migration; start Q3 2026

**What This Enables**
We can scale to 5x current capacity without additional capital. We gain modern tooling and reduce operational burden on our infrastructure team.

**Decision Needed**
Please confirm approval by end of this week so we can secure vendor commitments by 30 June.

Questions? Happy to discuss.

---

**Key principles shown:**
- Bottom line in first sentence
- Facts bulleted, not paragraphed
- Clear deadline
- Specific ask
- Length: under 250 words
