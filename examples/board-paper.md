# Board Paper Example (Condensed)

**BOARD PAPER: Acquisition Opportunity – DataVis Analytics**

**Executive Summary**
We recommend acquiring DataVis Analytics for £8M. This acquisition adds £1.2M ARR in a complementary product, expands our customer base into manufacturing, and accelerates our data visualisation roadmap by 12 months. Acquisition cost represents 6.7x revenue multiple; industry average is 8–10x.

**Background**
DataVis is a profitable data visualisation platform with 200 customers in manufacturing and logistics. Our platform lacks visualisation capabilities; we've received repeated customer requests. Building equivalent features would cost £2M and take 18 months.

**Key Metrics**
• DataVis ARR: £1.2M (growing 40% YoY)
• Gross margin: 72%
• Customer base: 200 (minimal overlap with our 150 customers)
• Management team: Staying post-acquisition

**Recommendation**
Acquire DataVis for £8M. This is lower cost and faster than building equivalent features internally.

**Risks & Mitigation**
• Integration risk: Hiring an integration lead; 90-day detailed plan
• Customer churn: Assigning account managers; ensuring continuity
• Cultural fit: Keeping management team; gradual integration

**Approval Requested**
Board approval to proceed with acquisition at £8M valuation.

---

**Key principles shown:**
- Executive summary is complete on its own
- Options analysis (build vs. buy) implicit
- Quantified metrics throughout
- Risks identified with mitigation
- Clear decision requested
