# Executive Update Example

**Q2 2026 Executive Update – Strong Growth with Rising Competition**

**Financial Performance**
• Revenue: £3.2M (23% above forecast; 40% YoY growth)
• Gross margin: 70% (up 2 points YoY)
• Churn: 1.2% (improved from 2.1%)
• New ARR: £200k (net of churn)

**What Drove Success**
Enterprise segment acceleration: Three major customers signed (£800k ARR total). Strong retention in mid-market (4% churn down from 6%). Product improvements resonating with customers.

**Challenges Emerging**
Competitive pressure increasing; two competitors launched at lower price points. One major customer delayed renewal decision (£200k ARR); outcome TBD by end July. Sales cycle lengthening in new markets.

**Strategic Priorities Ahead**

**Q3 Focus:**
1. Secure delayed customer renewal (mitigate risk)
2. Launch new product feature (competitive advantage)
3. Expand to EMEA market (exploratory mode)

**Resources Needed**
£400k investment in competitive sales capability for Q3.

**Confidence & Risks**
We're tracking well against annual targets. Main risk is competitive pricing pressure eroding margins; we're evaluating pricing strategy.

---

**Key principles shown:**
- Opens with strong metrics
- Challenges acknowledged (not hidden)
- Clear priorities stated
- Specific resource ask
- Honest assessment of risks
- Appropriate for busy executives
