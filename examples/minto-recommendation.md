# Minto Pyramid Recommendation Example

**RECOMMENDATION**
We should migrate engineering to a four-day work week and consolidate to a single office location.

**Why This Is Right**

**Reason 1: Cost Efficiency**
Current split between two offices (40% utilisation at each) wastes £400k annually on lease costs. Consolidating to one office saves £400k/year immediately. A four-day week reduces facility costs by 20% (fewer seats needed).

**Reason 2: Team Cohesion**
Remote-heavy work has created knowledge silos. In-person collaboration improves problem-solving and mentorship. Four days in-office maintains collaboration benefit whilst respecting flexibility preferences (surveys show 73% support flexible arrangements).

**Reason 3: Recruitment Advantage**
A four-day week is a recruiting advantage in competitive markets. It positions us as forward-thinking and improves hiring velocity for senior engineers (+30% in pilot group).

**Implications**
Implementation timeline: 3 months (office consolidation, policy communication, process changes). This requires active change management. We should launch with pilot group first (engineering team).

---

**Key principles shown:**
- Recommendation stated upfront
- Exactly 3 supporting reasons
- Each reason has evidence
- Logical flow: Cost, Culture, Talent
- Clear implications and next steps
