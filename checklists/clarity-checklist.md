# Checklist: Clarity Review

Review any document for clarity before publishing.

## Language Clarity

- [ ] No jargon without explanation
- [ ] No acronyms without spelling out on first use
- [ ] Sentences under 20 words
- [ ] One idea per sentence
- [ ] Active voice (not passive)
- [ ] Specific words (not vague: "40%" not "significant")

## Structure Clarity

- [ ] Headline or opening is clear
- [ ] Paragraphs are short (3–4 sentences max)
- [ ] Headings break content into scannable sections
- [ ] Lists are bulleted, not paragraphed
- [ ] Tables have clear headers
- [ ] Conclusion or ask is explicit

## Reader Experience

- [ ] Can reader understand in 30 seconds?
- [ ] Key points are obvious (not buried)
- [ ] Supporting facts are bulleted or numbered
- [ ] Action items are clear
- [ ] Tone is appropriate for audience
- [ ] No unnecessary words or elaboration

## Final Check

- [ ] Read aloud; does it sound natural?
- [ ] A 12-year-old could understand this?
- [ ] Someone skimming gets the key point?
- [ ] All claims are specific, not vague?

---

When in doubt: Simpler is better.
