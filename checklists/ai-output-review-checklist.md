# Checklist: AI Output Review

Review before using AI-generated content in professional contexts.

## Accuracy Check

- [ ] All facts are accurate and current
- [ ] No hallucinations or made-up references
- [ ] Numbers and statistics are verified
- [ ] No fabricated citations
- [ ] Technical claims are correct
- [ ] Names and titles are accurate
- [ ] No invented URLs, case names, statutory references, product features, or benchmark figures
- [ ] Facts and assumptions are visibly separate

## Clarity Check

- [ ] Content matches your voice and tone
- [ ] Language is appropriate for audience
- [ ] No awkward or overly formal phrasing
- [ ] Flows naturally
- [ ] Main points are clear
- [ ] No redundant information
- [ ] British English used throughout where required
- [ ] Missing critical information is flagged rather than hidden

## Completeness Check

- [ ] All required content is present
- [ ] No important sections are missing
- [ ] Examples support the message
- [ ] Sufficient detail without over-elaboration
- [ ] Actionable (not just informational)
- [ ] Risks, caveats, and trade-offs are included where relevant
- [ ] The output can be used as an artefact, not just inspiration

## Professional Standards

- [ ] No grammatical or spelling errors
- [ ] Appropriate length
- [ ] Proper formatting
- [ ] Consistent style throughout
- [ ] Suitable for intended use
- [ ] High-impact output has been reviewed against the relevant rubric in [rubrics/](../rubrics/)

## Originality Check

- [ ] Not copying existing copyrighted material
- [ ] Unique perspective or approach
- [ ] Appropriate attribution if referencing others
- [ ] Reflects your actual experience/knowledge

## Final Judgment

- [ ] Would you put your name on this?
- [ ] Is it ready for intended audience?
- [ ] Does it represent your standards?
- [ ] Any edits needed?

---

AI is a writing tool, not a replacement for your judgment and verification.
