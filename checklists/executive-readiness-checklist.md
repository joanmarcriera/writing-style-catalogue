# Checklist: Executive Readiness

Review before sharing with executive leadership.

## Content Readiness

- [ ] One-sentence headline captures the story
- [ ] Executive summary is 1 page max
- [ ] Key points are bulleted, not paragraphed
- [ ] All claims backed by data or facts
- [ ] No vague language ("seems," "might," "relatively")
- [ ] Recommendation or ask is explicit and clear
- [ ] Timeline is specific (not "soon" or "eventually")

## Structure Readiness

- [ ] Bottom line or recommendation appears upfront
- [ ] Supporting facts are grouped logically
- [ ] Risks are identified and mitigated
- [ ] No surprises (key points were likely already discussed)
- [ ] Decision or approval needed is explicit

## Professional Readiness

- [ ] Grammar and spelling are flawless
- [ ] Formatting is consistent
- [ ] No typos or obvious errors
- [ ] Tone is professional but accessible
- [ ] Document looks polished (not draft)
- [ ] Links and references work

## Context Readiness

- [ ] Assumes no special context (self-contained)
- [ ] No unexplained acronyms or jargon
- [ ] Appropriate level of detail for audience
- [ ] Decision or ask is front and centre

---

If it's not ready for the CEO, it's not ready.
