# Checklist: Email Quality Review

Before sending professional emails.

## Content

- [ ] Subject line is clear and specific
- [ ] First sentence gets to the point
- [ ] Key facts are bulleted, not paragraphed
- [ ] Ask is explicit and specific
- [ ] Deadline is clear (if applicable)
- [ ] Tone is professional but conversational
- [ ] No undefined acronyms

## Structure

- [ ] Paragraphs are short (2–3 sentences max)
- [ ] Action items are obvious
- [ ] Easy to scan in 30 seconds
- [ ] Next steps or follow-up is clear
- [ ] Signature block is complete

## Proofreading

- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] Names and titles are spelled correctly
- [ ] All links are working
- [ ] Attachments mentioned and included

## Tone Check

- [ ] Appropriate for recipient and context
- [ ] Not too formal, not too casual
- [ ] No unnecessary apologies or hedging
- [ ] Confident but respectful
- [ ] No ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!

---

Simple check: Would you be comfortable if this was forwarded to the CEO?
