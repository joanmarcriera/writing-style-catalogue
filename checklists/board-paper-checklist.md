# Checklist: Board Paper Review

Before submitting a board paper for governance review.

## Content

- [ ] Executive summary is 1 page; captures all essentials
- [ ] Background clearly establishes why this decision matters
- [ ] All options presented objectively (not biased toward recommendation)
- [ ] Financial impact quantified for each option
- [ ] Risks explicitly identified with mitigation strategies
- [ ] Recommendation is clear and justified
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable

## Structure

- [ ] Table of contents if >10 pages
- [ ] Consistent heading hierarchy
- [ ] Appendices support main narrative
- [ ] Page numbers and date visible
- [ ] Appendices are labelled clearly

## Professional Standards

- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] All charts and tables are clear and labelled
- [ ] Financial figures are consistent throughout
- [ ] Legal or compliance implications addressed
- [ ] References or sources cited where needed

## Governance Readiness

- [ ] Board knows what decision is being asked
- [ ] All material information is included
- [ ] No major surprises (pre-discussion happened)
- [ ] Timeline for decision is clear
- [ ] Approval language is specific

---

Board papers should be self-contained and decision-ready.
