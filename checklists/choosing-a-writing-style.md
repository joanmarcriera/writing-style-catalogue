# Checklist: Choosing a Writing Style

Use this checklist to select the right writing style for your document.

## Step 1: Define Your Purpose

- [ ] What is the primary goal? (Update / Recommend / Teach / Decide / Record)
- [ ] Who will read this? (Executive / Team / Specialist / General)
- [ ] How much time do they have? (5 min / 30 min / 1+ hours)
- [ ] What action or decision is needed?

## Step 2: Match to Style

- [ ] Need to update quickly? → Smart Brevity or BLUF
- [ ] Need to recommend change? → Minto Pyramid or Consulting
- [ ] Need to teach or explain? → Plain English, Socratic, or Tutorial
- [ ] Need formal documentation? → Board Paper, Technical, or Legal
- [ ] Need to persuade or engage? → Narrative or Persuasive Sales

## Step 3: Verify Fit

- [ ] Does the style match the audience expertise level?
- [ ] Is the length appropriate for the urgency?
- [ ] Does the style support the key message?
- [ ] Would the audience expect this style for this document?

## Step 4: Plan Your Structure

- [ ] What are the 3–5 key points?
- [ ] In what order should they appear?
- [ ] What supporting data or evidence is needed?
- [ ] What action or decision are you requesting?
