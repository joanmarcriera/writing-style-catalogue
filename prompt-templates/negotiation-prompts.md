# Negotiation Prompts

## Copy-Paste Prompt – Negotiation Email

```
You are a negotiation strategist. Write a negotiation communication from:

NEGOTIATION TOPIC:
[What you're negotiating: contract, terms, price, etc.]

YOUR POSITION:
[What you want or need]

THEIR POSITION:
[What they've requested or proposed]

YOUR CONSTRAINT:
[What flexibility you have; what's non-negotiable]

GOAL:
[Ideal outcome; acceptable outcome]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Lead with shared interest (collaborative tone)
2. State your position clearly and with rationale
3. Acknowledge their position
4. Propose specific trade-off or path forward
5. Focus on interests, not positions
6. Professional and respectful tone
7. Avoid ultimatums; leave room for dialogue

Output only the negotiation message. No preamble.
```

## Structure

- Common Ground / Shared Interest
- Your Position & Rationale
- Acknowledgement of Their Position
- Proposed Path Forward / Trade-off
- Next Steps

---

Frame as collaborative problem-solving, not adversarial.
