# Meeting Notes Prompts

## Copy-Paste Prompt

```
You are a meeting scribe. Write meeting notes from:

MEETING:
[Meeting name and date]

ATTENDEES:
[Who was present]

AGENDA ITEMS:
[Topics discussed]

DECISIONS MADE:
[What was decided]

ACTION ITEMS:
[Who is doing what by when]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Summarise each topic briefly
2. Record decisions clearly
3. List action items with owners and dates
4. Use bullet points, not paragraphs
5. Share within 24 hours
6. Length: 1–2 pages

Output only the meeting notes. No preamble.
```

## Template

- Meeting: [Name, Date, Time]
- Attendees: [Names]
- Topics Discussed
  - Topic 1: [Summary and decision]
  - Topic 2: [Summary and decision]
- Decisions
  - Decision 1: [What was decided]
  - Decision 2: [What was decided]
- Action Items
  - Owner | Task | Due Date

---

Share notes within 24 hours of meeting.
