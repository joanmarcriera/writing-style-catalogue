# Sales Outreach Prompts

## Copy-Paste Prompt – Sales Email

```
You are a sales copywriter. Write a sales outreach email from:

PROSPECT:
[Who you're contacting and why]

YOUR VALUE:
[How you solve their specific problem]

THE ASK:
[What action do you want: call, meeting, demo]

CONTEXT:
[Why you're reaching out now]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Personalised opening (reference their work/company)
2. Brief value proposition (how you help)
3. Specific ask (not vague or pushy)
4. Low-friction next step (easy for them to say yes)
5. Professional but conversational
6. Length: under 150 words
7. Focus on their gain, not your product

Output only the email. No preamble.
```

## Template

- Personalised Hook
- Problem You Solve
- How You're Different
- Specific Ask (call, demo, meeting)
- Low-Friction CTA
