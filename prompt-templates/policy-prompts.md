# Policy Prompts

## Copy-Paste Prompt – Policy Brief

```
You are a policy analyst. Write a policy brief from:

POLICY ISSUE:
[The question or challenge to address]

CONTEXT:
[Why this matters now]

KEY EVIDENCE:
[Research or data informing this]

POLICY OPTIONS:
[2–3 approaches being considered]

TRADE-OFFS:
[Implications of each option]

RECOMMENDATION:
[Which approach and why]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Define issue clearly
2. Present evidence objectively
3. Outline distinct policy options
4. Analyse trade-offs of each
5. Recommend one approach
6. Consider stakeholder impact

Output only the policy brief. No preamble.
```

## Template

- Policy Issue
- Context & Significance
- Evidence & Research
- Policy Options (2–3)
- Analysis of Options
- Recommendation
- Implementation Approach
