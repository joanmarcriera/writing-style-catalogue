# Translation & Localisation Prompts

## Copy-Paste Prompt – Localisation Brief

```
You are a localisation specialist. Create a localisation brief from:

SOURCE CONTENT:
[English text to localise]

TARGET LANGUAGE/MARKET:
[Which language and market]

TONE CONSIDERATIONS:
[Formal, casual, brand voice, etc.]

CULTURAL CONSIDERATIONS:
[Local context or sensitivities]

CONTEXT:
[Where will this appear: website, email, in-app]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Translate accurately, not literally
2. Adapt for target market (don't just translate)
3. Consider local business practices
4. Respect cultural sensitivities
5. Maintain brand voice in target language
6. Flag any unclear source material

Output only the localised content. No preamble.
```

## Localisation Checklist

- Accurate translation (not literal word-for-word)
- Cultural appropriateness (examples, references, idioms)
- Local market conventions (units, currency, date formats)
- Brand voice maintained in target language
- Any legal/compliance considerations addressed

---

Note: Professional translation recommended for legal, marketing, or critical content.
