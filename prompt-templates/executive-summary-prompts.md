# Executive Summary Prompts

## Purpose

Write concise summaries for executive or board-level audiences.

## Copy-Paste Prompt – Executive Summary

```
You are an executive summary expert. Write a summary from:

FULL DOCUMENT OR SITUATION:
[Paste the detailed content to summarise]

KEY AUDIENCE:
[C-suite, board, investors]

LENGTH:
[1–2 pages maximum]

CRITICAL FACTS:
[What they absolutely must know]

RECOMMENDATION OR ASK:
[What decision or action do you need]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Headline: capture the story in one sentence
2. No jargon or technical detail unless essential
3. Lead with key facts and decision needed
4. Use bullet points, not paragraphs
5. Include financial or strategic impact
6. End with clear ask

Output only the executive summary. No preamble.
```

## Variables to Customise

- `[FULL DOCUMENT]` → Detailed source material
- `[KEY AUDIENCE]` → Who will read it
- `[CRITICAL FACTS]` → Must-know information
- `[RECOMMENDATION]` → What action/decision

## Example Output (Smart Brevity Style)

**Quarterly Performance Summary – Revenue Growth 23%**

• YTD revenue: £4.2M (up 23% YoY)
• Customer base: 150 (growth 18% from start of year)
• Gross margin: 68% (stable vs. prior year)

**Key drivers**: New product uptake in enterprise segment, seasonal Q2 improvement, improved unit economics in core market.

**Headwinds**: Competitive pricing pressure in mid-market, one major customer churn (£200k), higher-than-expected support costs.

**Next steps**: Approve £600k investment in enterprise sales. Projected payback within 18 months.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Headline captures the core story
- [ ] Bullet points, not paragraphs
- [ ] No unexplained jargon
- [ ] Financial/strategic impact included
- [ ] Clear ask or recommendation
- [ ] Length under 2 pages
- [ ] Scannable in under 5 minutes
