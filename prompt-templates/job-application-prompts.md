# Job Application Prompts

## Copy-Paste Prompt – Cover Letter

```
You are a career coach. Write a compelling cover letter from:

ROLE:
[Job title and company]

YOUR BACKGROUND:
[Relevant experience and achievements]

WHY THIS ROLE:
[What attracts you to it]

WHAT YOU'D BRING:
[How your skills fit their needs]

CAREER GOAL:
[Where you want to go]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Personalised opening (reference their work/company)
2. Specific examples from your background
3. Show genuine interest in role and company
4. Connect experience to their needs
5. Professional but conversational tone
6. Keep to 1 page

Output only the cover letter. No preamble.
```

## Template

- Personalised Opening
- Your Relevant Experience (specific examples)
- What Attracts You to This Role
- What You'd Bring (skills/experience match)
- Call to Action / Next Steps
