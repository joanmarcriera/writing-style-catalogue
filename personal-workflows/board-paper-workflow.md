# Workflow: Creating a Board Paper

Multi-week process for major governance documents.

## Phase 1: Planning (Week 1)

**Step 1: Define the Decision**
- What decision does the board need to make?
- Why is timing urgent?
- What information does the board need?

**Step 2: Gather Supporting Data**
- Financial models and projections
- Competitive or market analysis
- Operational implications
- Risk assessment
- Legal or compliance considerations

**Step 3: Identify Options**
- What are 2–3 viable paths forward?
- Why each option (rationale)?
- What are pros/cons of each?

## Phase 2: Draft (Week 2–3)

**Use Prompt**: [Board Paper Prompt](../prompt-templates/board-paper-prompts.md)

**Structure**:
1. Executive Summary (1 page)
2. Background (2 pages)
3. Option Analysis (4–5 pages)
4. Recommendation (1 page)
5. Risk Assessment (2 pages)
6. Implementation (1 page)
7. Appendices (supporting data)

**Style**: [Board Paper](../styles/board-paper-style.md)

## Phase 3: Internal Review (Week 3)

- CFO review (financial accuracy)
- General counsel review (legal/compliance)
- Operational leads (feasibility)
- Incorporate feedback

## Phase 4: Pre-Board Discussion (Week 3)

- Brief board chair on contents
- Ensure no surprises in full board meeting
- Gather any pre-meeting questions

## Phase 5: Final Polish (Week 4)

**Checklist**: [Board Paper Checklist](../checklists/board-paper-checklist.md)

- Grammar and spelling ✓
- Consistent formatting ✓
- All charts/tables clear ✓
- Professional appearance ✓
- Ready for printing/distribution ✓

## Phase 6: Board Meeting

- Present executive summary (5 min)
- Open for discussion
- Facilitate decision

---

**Total time: 3–4 weeks**

**Frequency**: As needed for major decisions

**Key Success Factors:**
- Clear decision being asked
- Objective analysis of options
- No surprises (pre-discussion happened)
- Financial accuracy
- Professional presentation
