# Workflow: Supplier or Importer Outreach

Process for contacting potential suppliers or importers with business proposals.

## Step 1: Identify & Research Prospects (2–3 hours)

- Find potential suppliers/importers via:
  - Industry directories
  - Trade associations
  - LinkedIn or company websites
  - Referrals from network
- Research each prospect:
  - Company size and growth
  - Product/service offerings
  - Recent news or activity
  - Decision-makers and roles
  - Pain points they might have

## Step 2: Personalise Approach (1 hour per prospect)

- What specific value can you offer them?
- How does your offering align with their business?
- Who is the right person to contact?
- What's the best way to reach them?

## Step 3: Craft Outreach Message (1–2 hours total)

**Use Prompt**: [Sales Outreach Prompt](../prompt-templates/sales-outreach-prompts.md)

**Structure**:
- Opening: Reference something specific about their business
- Value proposition: How you help them specifically
- Specific ask: What you want (call, meeting, sample)
- Easy next step: Low-friction way to say yes

**Style**: [Persuasive Sales](../styles/persuasive-sales-style.md)

**Length**: Under 150 words (email or LinkedIn)

## Step 4: Send Initial Outreach

- Email to relevant contact
- Or LinkedIn message
- Or phone call (if relationship exists)
- Personalise each message (don't use templated bulk emails)

## Step 5: Follow-up Sequence (1–2 weeks)

**Email 1**: Initial outreach
**Email 2** (Day 3–4): Light follow-up if no response
**Email 3** (Day 7–10): Different angle or value prop
**Email 4** (Day 14): Last attempt or reference/testimonial

See [Email Sequence Skill](../skills/create-email-sequence.md)

## Step 6: Meetings & Negotiation

If interest shown:
- Schedule discovery call
- Prepare meeting materials
- Use [Negotiation Prompts](../prompt-templates/negotiation-prompts.md) for contract discussions
- Document outcomes

---

**Success Metrics**:
- Response rate (target: 10–15% for cold outreach)
- Meeting conversion (target: 20–30% of responses)
- Deal conversion (highly variable; depends on offering)

**Key Success Factors**:
- Research before reaching out
- Personalise messages (don't bulk send)
- Lead with their benefit, not your product
- Make it easy to say yes
- Follow up professionally
