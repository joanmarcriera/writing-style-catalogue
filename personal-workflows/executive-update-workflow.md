# Workflow: Creating an Executive Update

End-to-end process for creating quarterly or periodic executive updates.

## Step 1: Gather Information (2 hours)

- Collect financial metrics (revenue, growth, margins, churn)
- Document achievements this period
- Identify challenges or headwinds
- Note strategic priorities for next period
- Compile supporting data (customer wins, product launches, team changes)

**Template to Use**: None (gather raw data first)

## Step 2: Identify Key Messages (1 hour)

- What's the headline? (revenue up? growth accelerating? challenges emerging?)
- What 3–5 facts matter most?
- What challenges should leadership know about?
- What do you need from leadership (approval, resources, decisions)?

**Style to Use**: [Smart Brevity](../styles/axios-smart-brevity.md) or [Investor Update](../styles/investor-update-style.md)

## Step 3: Draft the Update (2 hours)

**Use Prompt**: [Executive Summary Prompt](../prompt-templates/executive-summary-prompts.md)

Structure:
- Headline (one compelling sentence)
- Key metrics (3–5 bulleted facts)
- Context or explanation
- Challenges ahead
- Next quarter priorities
- Ask (if applicable)

## Step 4: Review & Refine (1 hour)

**Checklist**: [Executive Readiness](../checklists/executive-readiness-checklist.md)

- Headline captures story? ✓
- Facts are specific, not vague? ✓
- Length is appropriate (1–2 pages)? ✓
- Ask is clear? ✓
- Ready for leadership? ✓

## Step 5: Share & Gather Feedback (2 hours)

- Share with CFO or COO for input
- Gather feedback; incorporate
- Finalise before distribution

---

**Total time: 6–8 hours**

**Frequency**: Monthly or quarterly

**Key Success Factors:**
- Balanced (not hiding challenges)
- Backed by data
- Clear ask or priority
