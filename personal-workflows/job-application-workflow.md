# Workflow: Job Application Process

Complete process for crafting strong job applications.

## Step 1: Research (1–2 hours)

- Read job description thoroughly
- Research company (business, culture, recent news)
- Research hiring manager if possible
- Identify 3–5 ways your experience matches their needs
- Note specific requirements and nice-to-haves

## Step 2: Personalise (1 hour)

- Tailor CV/resume to this role (emphasise relevant experience)
- Identify specific examples that match job description
- Prepare specific reasons why this role appeals to you

## Step 3: Draft Cover Letter (2–3 hours)

**Use Prompt**: [Job Application Prompt](../prompt-templates/job-application-prompts.md)

**Structure**:
- Opening: Why this role appeals to you specifically
- Experience: 2–3 relevant examples with metrics
- What you'd bring: How your skills fit their needs
- Call to action: Specific next step

**Style**: [Job Application](../styles/job-application-style.md)

**Length**: One page maximum

## Step 4: Review Before Sending (1 hour)

**Checklist**: [Clarity Checklist](../checklists/clarity-checklist.md)

- Spelling and grammar perfect? ✓
- Personalised to this specific role? ✓
- Examples are specific, not generic? ✓
- Shows research of company? ✓
- Tone is authentic and confident? ✓
- Ready to send? ✓

## Step 5: Submit

- Send to specified contact or via application portal
- Follow any specific submission instructions
- Keep records of applications (company, date, contact)

## Step 6: Follow-up (if applicable)

- Wait 1–2 weeks before following up
- Send brief, polite check-in email if no response
- Reference your original application

---

**Total time: 5–6 hours per application**

**Key Success Factors**:
- Thoroughly research the company
- Personalise (don't send generic letters)
- Show enthusiasm with specific examples
- Address their needs, not just your wants
- Proofread carefully (first impression matters)
