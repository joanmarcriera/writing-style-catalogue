# Workflow: One-Page Study or Learning Summary

Quick process for capturing key insights from learning (book, course, research).

## Step 1: Read/Study (Variable)

- Work through the material
- Take notes on key ideas
- Mark important passages or concepts
- Note examples and applications

## Step 2: Identify Key Insights (30 min)

- What are the 3–5 core ideas?
- What changed your thinking?
- What surprised you?
- What's immediately applicable?
- What do you want to remember?

## Step 3: Organise Insights (30 min)

**Structure**:
- Title & Source
- Main Thesis (one sentence)
- Key Concept 1 (with explanation and example)
- Key Concept 2 (with explanation and example)
- Key Concept 3 (with explanation and example)
- How I'll Apply This
- Unanswered Questions

## Step 4: Draft Summary (1–2 hours)

**Use Prompt**: [Learning Notes Prompt](../prompt-templates/learning-notes-prompts.md)

**Style**: [Plain English](../styles/plain-english.md) or [Socratic Explainer](../styles/socratic-explainer.md)

**Length**: Exactly one page (constraints force clarity)

## Step 5: Refine (30 min)

- Is each concept explained clearly?
- Are examples concrete and relevant?
- Does it fit on one page?
- Is the "how I'll apply this" specific and actionable?
- Would I understand this 6 months from now?

## Step 6: File & Reference (10 min)

- Save with source reference
- Add to personal knowledge base
- Note where you might apply it
- Review periodically

---

**Total time: 3–4 hours**

**Key Success Factors**:
- Explain concepts in your own words (not copying)
- Include specific examples
- Focus on what's actionable
- Use constraint (one page) to force clarity
- Review summaries periodically to cement learning

**One-pagers become your personal learning library. Build it over time.**
