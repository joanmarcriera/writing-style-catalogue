# Workflow: Creating a Tax Technical Note

Process for producing professional tax analyses.

## Step 1: Clarify the Question (30 min)

- Get client's specific tax question in writing
- Identify jurisdictions involved
- Understand business context
- Note any time constraints
- Identify background assumptions

## Step 2: Research (2–3 hours)

- Review relevant statutes and regulations
- Check HMRC guidance or local equivalents
- Research case law and precedent
- Identify any areas of uncertainty
- Gather all facts from client

## Step 3: Analyse (2–3 hours)

- Apply law to facts
- Identify alternative interpretations
- Consider risk levels for each view
- Document analysis thoroughly
- Note any grey areas or uncertainties

## Step 4: Draft the Note (1–2 hours)

**Use Prompt**: [Tax Note Prompt](../prompt-templates/tax-note-prompts.md)

**Structure**:
- Question (clear statement)
- Facts (relevant circumstances)
- Analysis (application of law)
- Conclusion (clear answer; confidence level)
- Next Steps (documentation, further actions)

**Style**: [Tax Advisory](../styles/tax-advisory-style.md)

## Step 5: Review & Verify (1 hour)

**Checklist**: [Tax Note Checklist](../checklists/tax-note-checklist.md)

- Law applied correctly? ✓
- Facts are accurate? ✓
- Analysis is sound? ✓
- Conclusion follows logically? ✓
- Confidence level appropriate? ✓
- Professional standards met? ✓

## Step 6: Internal Review (1–2 hours)

- Senior tax adviser reviews
- Verify legal positions
- Incorporate feedback
- Finalise

## Step 7: Client Delivery

- Send to client with covering letter
- Offer follow-up discussion
- Document client's response/acceptance

---

**Total time: 8–12 hours**

**Frequency**: Per client request

**Key Success Factors:**
- Question clearly stated
- Law correctly applied
- Facts verified
- Uncertainties acknowledged
- Professional standards maintained
- Qualified review before delivery
