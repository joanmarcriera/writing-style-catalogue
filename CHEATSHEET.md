# Style Cheatsheet

Quick reference for choosing the right writing style.

| Style | Best For | Length | Tone | Key Feature |
|-------|----------|--------|------|-------------|
| **Smart Brevity** | Executive updates, news | 1–2 pages | Direct, scannable | Headline + 3 key points |
| **BLUF** | Emails, memos | 1 page | Professional, action-focused | Bottom line in first sentence |
| **Minto Pyramid** | Recommendations, strategy | 5–10 pages | Analytical, logical | Conclusion first, evidence below |
| **Plain English** | Instructions, guides | Flexible | Clear, jargon-free | Simple words, short sentences |
| **Executive Briefing** | C-suite presentations | 2–3 pages | Formal, polished | Context + recommendation + risk |
| **Consulting Style** | Strategic memos | 5–10 pages | Authoritative, structured | Issue, approach, recommendation |
| **Board Paper** | Board meetings | 10–20 pages | Formal, thorough | Background + options + decision |
| **Technical Documentation** | Technical audience | 10–50 pages | Precise, complete | Explanation + example + reference |
| **Tax Advisory** | Tax professionals | 5–15 pages | Precise, legal | Question + analysis + conclusion |
| **Legal Professional** | Legal documents | Flexible | Formal, exact | Defined terms + precise language |
| **Academic** | Research, papers | Flexible | Formal, evidence-based | Thesis + argument + evidence |
| **Journalistic** | News, public comms | 500–2000 words | Engaging, factual | Hook + story + sources |
| **Persuasive Sales** | Sales emails, pitches | 1–3 pages | Compelling, benefit-focused | Problem + solution + value |
| **Narrative Storytelling** | Internal comms, vision | 2–5 pages | Engaging, human | Story arc + lesson + call-to-action |
| **Socratic Explainer** | Teaching, learning | 3–10 pages | Conversational, inquisitive | Questions + answers + deeper questions |
| **Tutorial** | How-to guides | 5–20 pages | Step-by-step, encouraging | Intro + steps + examples + troubleshooting |
| **Crisis Communications** | Emergency response | 1–2 pages | Calm, clear, factual | What happened + what we're doing + next steps |
| **Policy Brief** | Policy analysis | 5–10 pages | Balanced, evidence-based | Issue + analysis + recommendations |
| **Product Requirements** | PRDs, specs | 10–15 pages | Precise, comprehensive | Goal + requirements + acceptance criteria |
| **Architecture Decision Record** | Technical decisions | 2–5 pages | Structured, traceable | Context + decision + rationale |
| **Incident Report** | Post-mortems | 3–8 pages | Factual, learning-focused | Timeline + cause + actions |
| **Investor Update** | Stakeholder comms | 2–3 pages | Professional, optimistic | Performance + strategy + ask |
| **Performance Review** | Employee feedback | 1–2 pages | Constructive, specific | Strengths + development areas + goals |
| **Job Application** | Cover letters, LinkedIn | 1 page | Personal, compelling | Why + proof + call-to-action |
| **Social Media** | Twitter, LinkedIn, posts | 1–3 sentences | Conversational, punchy | Hook + point + CTA |

## Quick Selection Logic

**Need a quick update?** → Smart Brevity or BLUF

**Need to persuade?** → Minto Pyramid, Consulting Style, or Persuasive Sales

**Need to teach?** → Socratic Explainer, Tutorial, or Plain English

**Need to record a decision?** → Architecture Decision Record or Incident Report

**Need formal documentation?** → Board Paper, Technical Documentation, or Legal Professional

**Need to engage an audience?** → Narrative Storytelling, Journalistic, or Social Media
