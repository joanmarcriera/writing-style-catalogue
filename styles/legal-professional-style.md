# Legal Professional Style

## What This Style Is

Formal, precise legal writing. Uses defined terms, exact language, and careful structure. Designed to be legally defensible and clear.

**Core principle**: Exact language, no ambiguity, every term defined.

## When to Use It

- Contracts and agreements
- Legal memoranda
- Terms of service
- Privacy policies
- Compliance documentation
- Formal legal opinions

## When NOT to Use It

- Internal memos (use Consulting Style)
- Quick decisions (use BLUF)
- Public-facing materials (use Plain English)
- Educational content (use Socratic)

## Core Principles

**Use defined terms consistently**
- "The Company" always refers to the same entity
- Terms in quotes or bold are specially defined
- List definitions in preamble

**Use exact, unambiguous language**
- "Must" = required
- "May" = optional
- "Shall" = intended obligation
- Avoid: "should," "might," "potentially"

**Structure clearly**
- Articles and sections numbered
- Subsections clearly nested
- Cross-references explicit

**Use condition and consequence precisely**
- "If [condition], then [consequence]"
- "In the event that" is formal alternative

## Short Example

**Service Level Agreement (Extract)**

**1. Definitions**

"Service" means the cloud hosting service provided by the Provider to the Customer.

"Uptime" means the percentage of hours in a month during which the Service is available and functioning as specified.

"Support Hours" means 09:00 to 18:00 GMT, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

"Critical Incident" means any Service failure affecting 50% or more of Customer's users.

**2. Service Levels**

2.1 The Provider shall maintain Uptime of 99.5% per calendar month, measured across all availability zones.

2.2 Uptime excludes:
- (a) Scheduled maintenance (with 72 hours' notice)
- (b) Issues caused by Customer's configuration or data
- (c) Force majeure events beyond Provider's reasonable control

**3. Support**

3.1 The Provider shall:
- (a) Respond to Critical Incidents within 1 hour during Support Hours
- (b) Provide status updates every 2 hours
- (c) Document all incidents

3.2 Emergency support outside Support Hours is available at the rates specified in Schedule A.

**4. Credits**

4.1 If Uptime falls below 99.5% in a month, the Customer may claim service credits:

| Uptime | Credit |
|--------|--------|
| 99.0–99.4% | 10% monthly fee |
| 98.0–98.9% | 25% monthly fee |
| Below 98.0% | 50% monthly fee |

4.2 Credits are the Customer's sole remedy for Service failures.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] All key terms are defined
- [ ] Language is exact (must/may/shall used correctly)
- [ ] No ambiguous pronouns or references
- [ ] Sections are numbered and clearly structured
- [ ] Cross-references are accurate
- [ ] Conditions and consequences are explicit
- [ ] Drafted with qualified legal review in mind

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|----------------|-----|
| Undefined terms | Ambiguity creates disputes | Define all key terms upfront |
| Weak verbs | Legal effect unclear | Use "must," "may," or "shall" precisely |
| Ambiguous pronouns | Readers confused about who | Use defined terms; repeat noun not pronoun |
| Missing conditions | Unintended obligations | State "if...then" explicitly |
| Overly complex sentences | Hard to interpret | Break into shorter sentences |
