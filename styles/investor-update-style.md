# Investor Update Style

## What This Style Is

Structured update for investors or major stakeholders. Balances transparency with optimism. Covers performance, strategy, and asks.

**Core principle**: Performance → Strategy → Ask.

## When to Use It

- Quarterly investor updates
- Stakeholder briefings
- Annual reports
- Fundraising updates
- Board communications
- Major milestone announcements

## When NOT to Use It

- Operational updates (use Smart Brevity)
- Executive staff communications (use Consulting)
- Crisis response (use Crisis Communications)
- Formal governance documents (use Board Paper)

## Core Structure

```
[HEADLINE]
Key theme or achievement.

[PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS]
Key metrics: growth, efficiency, market.

[STRATEGIC UPDATES]
Progress on roadmap; market positioning.

[RISKS OR CHALLENGES]
Transparent about headwinds.

[LOOKING AHEAD]
Next quarter or year priorities.

[ASK]
What you need from investors/stakeholders.
```

## Copy-Paste Prompt Template

```
You are an investor relations professional. Write an update from:

REPORTING PERIOD:
[Quarter or year]

KEY METRICS:
[Revenue, growth rate, key metrics]

PROGRESS:
[Strategic milestones achieved]

CHALLENGES:
[What's harder than expected]

NEXT PRIORITIES:
[What's coming next]

FUNDING ASK:
[Do you need more capital; if so, what for]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Lead with headline achievement
2. Present metrics honestly
3. Acknowledge challenges transparently
4. Show strategic momentum
5. Be optimistic but realistic
6. Length: 2–3 pages

Output only the update. No preamble.
```

## Short Example

**Q1 2026 Investor Update – DataMesh Analytics**

**We're Scaling Faster Than Expected: 40% Revenue Growth, Expanding Into Enterprise**

**Performance Highlights**
- ARR: £2.1M (up 40% YoY)
- Customer count: 127 customers (up from 91 last quarter)
- NRR: 118% (net revenue retention)
- Churn: 2% monthly (industry average 5%)
- Gross margin: 72% (up from 68% last quarter)

**Strategic Progress**
- Launched enterprise tier; first two enterprise customers signed
- Product: Completed data lineage feature (top customer request)
- Team: Hired VP Sales; building enterprise sales motion
- Market: Recognised in Gartner Cool Vendors report

**Challenges**
- Competition increasing; new entrants in space
- Sales cycle for enterprise: longer than expected (6 months, not 4)
- Customer success team is stretched; investing in automation

**Next Quarter Priorities**
- Close 3–5 enterprise customers (path to £4M ARR)
- Launch customer data integrations (competitive advantage)
- Expand to EMEA market (explore London presence)

**Capital Ask**
We're raising a £3M Series A to accelerate sales and product. This funds:
- Enterprise sales team (3 heads)
- Product development (2 engineers)
- Customer success automation (1 head)
- Geographic expansion (EMEA team)

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Headline captures key achievement
- [ ] Metrics are honest and contextualised
- [ ] Challenges are acknowledged
- [ ] Strategy is clear and progressing
- [ ] Ask is specific and justified
- [ ] Tone is confident but realistic
- [ ] Length is appropriate (2–3 pages)

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|----------------|-----|
| Hiding challenges | Erodes investor trust | Be transparent; investors want realism |
| Overly optimistic | Credibility suffers | Temper optimism with honest challenges |
| Vague metrics | Investors can't assess progress | Provide specific, comparable numbers |
| No clear ask | Investors don't know what to do | Be explicit about what you need |
