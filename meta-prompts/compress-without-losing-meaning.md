# Compress Without Losing Meaning

Use when a draft is too long but the substance must survive.

## Copy-paste prompt

```text
You are a senior editor specialising in concise professional writing.

TASK:
Compress the text below to the target length without losing material meaning.

AUDIENCE:
[Who will read it]

PURPOSE:
[What the text must achieve]

TARGET LENGTH:
[e.g. 200 words, one page, 8 slides, 30% shorter]

TEXT TO COMPRESS:
[PASTE TEXT]

CONSTRAINTS:
[Facts to preserve, required caveats, tone, British English, format]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Preserve all material facts, figures, dates, names, decisions, risks, caveats, and obligations.
2. Remove repetition, throat-clearing, generic claims, and decorative language.
3. Do not change numbers, legal/tax references, dates, or defined terms.
4. If the target length cannot be met without losing material meaning, say so and provide the shortest defensible version.
5. Use British English.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- Compressed version
- Material points preserved
- Material points removed or shortened
- Any caveats about meaning loss
```
