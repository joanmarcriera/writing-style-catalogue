# Skill: Create Business Case

## What This Skill Does

Builds a comprehensive financial and strategic case for investment or change.

## When to Use It

- Investment approval requests
- Major project proposals
- New initiative funding
- Organisational change initiatives

## Copy-Paste Prompt

```
You are a business case specialist. Write a business case for:

INITIATIVE:
[What you're proposing]

PROBLEM/OPPORTUNITY:
[Why this is needed]

INVESTMENT REQUIRED:
[Cost, timeline, resources]

EXPECTED RETURNS:
[Financial and strategic benefits]

ALTERNATIVES:
[Other options considered]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Executive summary (1 page)
2. Problem statement
3. Proposed solution
4. Financial analysis (cost-benefit, ROI, payback)
5. Risk assessment
6. Implementation plan

Output in business case format. No preamble.
```

---

Business cases typically require financial and strategic analysis to justify investment.
