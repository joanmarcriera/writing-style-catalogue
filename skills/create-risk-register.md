# Skill: Create Risk Register

## What This Skill Does

Documents identified risks with assessment, mitigation, and ownership for ongoing monitoring.

## When to Use It

- Project risk management
- Operational risk tracking
- Board-level risk reporting
- Compliance and governance

## Copy-Paste Prompt

```
You are a risk specialist. Create a risk register for:

CONTEXT:
[What you're assessing risk for]

IDENTIFIED RISKS:
[List of potential risks]

RISK CRITERIA:
[Likelihood, impact, severity scales]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. List risks clearly
2. Assess likelihood and impact
3. Calculate risk score
4. Identify owner
5. Define mitigation strategy
6. Set review frequency

Output as table with columns: Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Owner | Mitigation
```

---

Update risk registers quarterly or after major changes.
