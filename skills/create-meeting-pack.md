# Skill: Create Meeting Pack

## What This Skill Does

Assembles all materials for a productive meeting including agenda, pre-read materials, and decision documents.

## When to Use It

- Client meetings
- Project kickoffs
- Strategy sessions
- Investor meetings

## Copy-Paste Prompt

```
You are a meeting coordinator. Create a meeting pack for:

MEETING:
[Type and purpose]

ATTENDEES:
[Who will be there]

AGENDA:
[Topics to cover]

DISCUSSION MATERIALS:
[What they need to review before]

DECISIONS NEEDED:
[Any decisions to be made]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Agenda with time allocations
2. Pre-read materials (concise)
3. Discussion frameworks or prompts
4. Decision templates
5. Next steps template
6. Parking lot for deferred issues

Output meeting pack outline. No preamble.
```

---

Effective meetings have good pre-read materials and clear decision frameworks.
