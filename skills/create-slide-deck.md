# Skill: Create Slide Deck

## What This Skill Does

Designs a complete slide deck outline with speaker notes, visuals specifications, and flow for presenting to an audience.

## When to Use It

- Planning a presentation or pitch
- Creating executive briefing materials
- Designing training or learning content
- Preparing for board or investor presentations

## Inputs Required

- Presentation topic and goal
- Target audience and duration
- Key points to cover
- Any supporting data or case studies

## Copy-Paste Prompt

```
You are a presentation designer. Create a slide deck outline from:

TOPIC:
[Presentation topic]

GOAL:
[What should audience do or believe after]

AUDIENCE:
[Who will see this]

DURATION:
[How long: 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour]

KEY POINTS:
[3–5 main messages]

SUPPORTING DATA:
[Facts, case studies, examples]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Slide-by-slide outline
2. 2–3 bullet points per slide
3. Speaker notes for each slide
4. Visual/diagram specifications
5. Timing guidance
6. Q&A slide

Output only the slide outline. No preamble.
```

## Suggested Output Structure

- Slide 1: Title
- Slide 2: Agenda
- Slides 3–5: Problem/Context
- Slides 6–9: Solution/Key Points
- Slide 10: Data/Evidence
- Slide 11: Call to Action
- Slide 12: Q&A

## Quality Controls

- [ ] Clear narrative arc (problem → solution → action)
- [ ] Each slide has one main idea
- [ ] Visual specifications are specific
- [ ] Speaker notes are clear and complete
- [ ] Slide count fits time allocation
- [ ] Call to action is clear
- [ ] Deck can be understood even without speaker

## Common Failure Modes

- Too many bullets per slide (overwhelms audience)
- No visual specifications (designer doesn't know what to create)
- Speaker notes missing (presenter unprepared)
- Call to action unclear or missing
- Narrative doesn't flow logically

## Example Request

"Create a 20-minute pitch deck for investors about a data analytics platform. Include problem, market opportunity, product overview, traction, team, and funding ask. Design for 15 slides with professional visuals."

---

Use PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, or Figma to execute outline.
