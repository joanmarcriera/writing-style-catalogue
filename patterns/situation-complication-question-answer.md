# Situation / Complication / Question / Answer

Use **SCQA** when you need to create a short strategic narrative. It gives the reader enough context to understand why the recommendation exists.

## Structure

```text
Situation: [Stable context the reader recognises]
Complication: [What changed, broke, emerged, or became urgent]
Question: [The decision or question now facing the reader]
Answer: [The recommendation or conclusion]
```

## Good for

- Strategy memos
- Board-paper executive summaries
- Consulting recommendations
- Business cases
- Slide deck openings

## Prompt line

```text
Open with the SCQA pattern: situation, complication, question, answer. Keep the answer explicit and do not bury it after the analysis.
```

## Quality check

- The situation is not too long.
- The complication creates genuine need for action.
- The question is answerable.
- The answer is clear enough to become a recommendation headline.
