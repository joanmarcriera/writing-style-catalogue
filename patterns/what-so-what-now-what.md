# What / So What / Now What

Use this pattern when raw facts need interpretation. It works especially well for updates, retrospectives, incident summaries, and executive briefings.

## Structure

```text
What: [What happened or what is true]
So what: [Why it matters, who is affected, what risk or opportunity it creates]
Now what: [What happens next, who owns it, and by when]
```

## Good for

- Weekly leadership updates
- IT incident summaries
- Project retrospectives
- Audit finding summaries
- Slide speaker notes

## Prompt line

```text
Use the What / So What / Now What pattern. Do not stop at description; explain the implication and finish with a concrete next action.
```

## Quality check

- "What" is factual, not interpretive.
- "So what" explains business impact, not just activity.
- "Now what" includes an owner and timing.
- Assumptions are labelled.
