# BLUF Pattern

**BLUF** means **Bottom Line Up Front**. Use it when the reader needs the answer immediately.

## Structure

```text
Bottom line: [Recommendation, answer, decision, or ask in one sentence]
Context: [Why this matters now, in one to three sentences]
Evidence: [Three to five bullets with the strongest supporting facts]
Action required: [What the reader must do, by whom, and by when]
Next step: [What happens after the action]
```

## Good for

- Approval emails
- CFO or CIO decision requests
- Escalations
- Status updates that need action
- Short recommendation memos

## Prompt line

```text
Use BLUF. Put the complete recommendation or ask in the first sentence. Then provide only the context, evidence, action required, and next step needed for the reader to act.
```

## Quality check

- The first sentence can stand alone.
- The ask is explicit.
- The deadline is visible.
- Supporting detail is specific and quantified where possible.
- There is only one primary ask.
