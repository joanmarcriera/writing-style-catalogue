# Website Marketing and SEO Rubric

Use this after generating a website audit, SEO report, content plan, conversion review, or 30/60/90-day website action plan.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope and evidence | Does not say what was inspected | Lists some pages or inputs | Clearly states URLs, data sources, tools, exclusions, and limitations |
| Fact integrity | Invents rankings, traffic, conversions, or analytics | Mostly avoids invented data but some claims are loose | Separates Observed, Inferred, and Not observed information throughout |
| Business relevance | Generic SEO advice | Some link to business goals | Recommendations directly support audience, offer, geography, and commercial goal |
| Technical SEO accuracy | Vague or incorrect technical claims | Covers basic metadata and crawl issues | Identifies crawl, indexability, rendering, schema, performance, redirects, canonical, sitemap, and mobile issues only where evidenced |
| Content and intent | Keyword list without rationale | Some audience and content gap analysis | Maps audience intent to page types, topic clusters, proof needs, and internal links |
| Conversion and messaging | Ignores the conversion path | Mentions CTA and page clarity | Diagnoses offer clarity, trust, proof, objections, form friction, journey steps, and CTA hierarchy |
| Prioritisation | Long undifferentiated list | Rough high/medium/low priorities | Uses impact, effort, confidence, dependency, owner, and sequencing |
| Measurement plan | No way to know if changes work | Mentions analytics generally | Defines events, KPIs, data access, baseline needs, and review cadence |
| Actionability | Advice is too vague to implement | Some actions are specific | Backlog items are owner-ready with evidence, recommendation, acceptance criteria, and next step |
| Professional judgement | Overconfident or salesy | Mostly practical | Clear caveats, realistic constraints, and British English with no inflated marketing language |

## Pass standard

For professional use, require:

- No criterion below **3**
- Fact integrity score of **5**
- Scope and evidence score of at least **4**
- Prioritisation score of at least **4**

Anything that invents traffic, rankings, conversions, keyword volume, backlink data, revenue, analytics findings, or private competitor performance cannot pass.

## Red flags

- "Your site ranks..." without Search Console, ranking tool, or supplied evidence.
- "Conversion rate is low..." without analytics or funnel data.
- "Backlinks are weak..." without backlink tool data.
- Recommendations that could apply to any website.
- No distinction between public-page observations and analytics-dependent findings.
- A backlog with no owner, dependency, or acceptance criteria.

## Review questions

- Can an executive understand the current state in five minutes?
- Can a delivery team start from the backlog without reinterpretation?
- Are quick wins separated from structural changes?
- Are measurement gaps handled before experiments?
- Are caveats visible enough to prevent false confidence?
