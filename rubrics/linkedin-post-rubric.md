# LinkedIn Post Rubric

Use this before scheduling or publishing a professional LinkedIn post.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | Generic announcement | Topic is clear | First two lines create a precise, credible reason to continue |
| Evidence | Unsupported claims | One concrete detail | Every factual claim maps to supplied evidence and caveats survive |
| Professional insight | Familiar platitude | Useful observation | Reader receives a specific, transferable working principle |
| Voice | Corporate or performative | Acceptably human | Direct, understated, recognisably authored, and free of hype |
| Mobile readability | Dense wall of text | Mostly scannable | Short paragraphs, deliberate white space, and restrained bullets |
| Call to conversation | “Thoughts?” | Relevant question | Closing question invites a specific professional experience or practice |
| Publication integrity | Links or names unchecked | Main details checked | Links, names, counts, claims, confidentiality, and permissions are verified |

## Pass standard

Evidence, professional insight, voice, and publication integrity must score at least **4**. No criterion may score below **3**.

## Red flags

- Invented engagement targets are presented as evidence of quality.
- Client or employer details are identifiable without permission.
- Hashtags or a call to action are used to compensate for a weak insight.
- The post claims a feature or public artefact that the linked page does not show.
