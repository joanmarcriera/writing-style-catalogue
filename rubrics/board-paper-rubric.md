# Board Paper Rubric

Use this before sending a board or committee paper for governance review.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision required | Unclear or absent | Present but not precise | Stated in the executive summary and proposed resolution |
| Executive summary | Too long or incomplete | Covers the main points | One-page maximum and sufficient for a director to understand the decision |
| Options analysis | One preferred option only | Options listed but uneven | Genuine options, including do nothing, assessed on the same criteria |
| Financial analysis | Missing or inconsistent | Includes headline costs | Comparable financials, assumptions, sensitivity, and funding source |
| Risk | Generic risk categories | Some specific risks | Specific risks with likelihood, impact, mitigation, and owner |
| Governance language | Informal or ambiguous | Mostly formal | Uses precise board language suitable for minutes and audit trail |
| Evidence and assumptions | Unsupported claims | Some assumptions stated | Facts, assumptions, projections, and recommendations are visibly separate |
| Implementation | Vague timing | Basic plan | Phases, owners, dependencies, milestones, and review gates |

## Pass standard

A board paper should score **4 or 5** for decision required, options analysis, financial analysis, and risk before distribution.

## Red flags

- The paper asks the board to approve without showing alternatives.
- The recommendation is advocated before options are fairly assessed.
- Financial figures differ between the executive summary, options table, and appendix.
- Legal, regulatory, or conflicts-of-interest issues are not addressed.
